Crystal Check

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-09

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Crystal Check ("the app", "we", "us"). In short: you don't need an account, we don't sell your data, we show no ads, and we don't keep your photos.

What stays on your phone

Your scans, photos, verdicts, home-test answers, collection, notes, tags and onboarding answers are stored only on your device. We don’t upload or back them up. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them, under Apple’s or Google’s terms.

Photos and screenshots you check

When you scan a stone or check a listing screenshot, the app first shrinks the image and removes its location and other hidden metadata on your phone. It is then sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to our AI provider to produce the result. Any details you add (what you were told the stone is, the price, where you bought it) are sent with it.

Before the first check, the app asks your permission to send photos to our AI provider. If you don’t agree, nothing is sent, and you can still use the encyclopedia, your collection and pasted-text listing checks.

We don’t store these images or the listing text after the result is returned. Our AI provider processes them only to send the result back to us, under its commercial terms, and doesn’t use them to train its models.

Anonymous account

The first time you open the app, it creates an anonymous account: a random ID with no name, email or phone number. It lets our server check that requests come from the app and count free uses: how many scans and checks you made each day, and whether you used the free Real-or-Fake check.

Technical logs

For each check our server records technical details: the time, how long it took, which AI model was used, how much AI processing it needed, error codes and which crystal was identified. These logs never contain your photos or listing text, and are deleted automatically after a short time.

Subscriptions

Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, linked to your anonymous ID. RevenueCat receives purchase details from Apple or Google, such as the product, dates, price, country and a store transaction ID. We never see your payment card details or your store account’s name or email.

Usage statistics

To improve the app we may record basic usage events with your anonymous ID, such as "scan completed" or which onboarding options were chosen, using PostHog’s EU servers. They never include photos, typed text, prices or crystal names you typed. We don’t use them to build advertising profiles.

Phone permissions

Camera: to photograph stones, only while you use the scanner. Photos: only the photos or screenshots you pick yourself. Notifications: only for the trial reminder below. You can change these permissions at any time in your phone’s settings. The app doesn’t access your location, contacts or microphone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a reminder on your phone 2 days before a free trial ends. It is created on your phone, not sent from our server. Turn it off in Settings → Trial reminder or in your phone’s settings.

Ratings

The app may show Apple’s or Google’s standard rating window. Your rating and review go to the store, not to us.

Why we process data

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) we rely on these legal bases: to provide the service you ask for (scans, listing checks, subscriptions); your consent (sending photos to our AI provider, notifications); and our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working, preventing abuse of the free limits, and improving the app with usage statistics. You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn’t affect processing that happened before.

How long we keep data

Photos and listing text: not kept after the result is returned. Usage counts: while your anonymous account exists; Delete all my data removes them. Technical logs: a short time, then deleted automatically. Purchase records: kept by Apple or Google and RevenueCat as long as needed for your subscription and as tax and accounting law requires. Data on your phone: until you delete it or the app.

What we never do

We don’t sell or rent your data, show ads, track you across other apps or websites, or access your location, contacts or microphone.

Service providers and transfers

Supabase (servers and anonymous accounts), Anthropic (AI analysis), RevenueCat (subscription status), PostHog (usage statistics, EU servers), and Apple and Google (app stores and payments). They process data only to provide their service to us.

Some providers process data in the United States. For transfers outside the European Economic Area we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as offered by each provider.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age for consent where you live (16 in some EU countries), and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices and rights

Settings → Delete all my data deletes everything stored on your phone, and on our server deletes your anonymous ID and its usage counts. Deleting the app removes what is on your phone. Your purchase history stays with Apple or Google and RevenueCat so your subscription keeps working; the app links it to your new anonymous ID.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you have rights to access, correct, delete or move your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the app doesn’t ask who you are, most data is on your phone, where you control it. To use your rights, contact us at the address below.

If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Romania that is the ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. AI keys and other secrets are kept only on our server, never in the app. Access to our server and provider accounts is limited and protected.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
