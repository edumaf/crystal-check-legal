Crystal Check

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-08

Crystal Check is made by Maftei Eduard-Ionut ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. In short: we don't sell your data, we show no ads, and we don't keep your photos.

What stays on your phone

Your scans, photos, verdicts, collection, notes, tags and onboarding answers are stored only on your device. We don’t upload or back them up. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them, under Apple’s or Google’s terms.

Photos and screenshots you check

When you scan a stone or check a listing screenshot, the app first shrinks the image and removes its location and other hidden metadata on your phone. It is then sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to our AI provider to produce the result. Any details you add (what you were told the stone is, the price, where you bought it) are sent with it.

We don’t store these images or the listing text after the result is returned. Our AI provider processes them only to send the result back to us, under its commercial terms.

Anonymous account

The first time you open the app, it creates an anonymous account: a random ID with no name, email or phone number. It lets our server check that requests come from the app and count free uses: how many scans and checks you made each day, and whether you used the free Real-or-Fake check.

Technical logs

For each check our server records technical details: the time, how long it took, which AI model was used, how much AI processing it needed, error codes and which crystal was identified. These logs never contain your photos or listing text, and are kept only for a limited time to keep the service working.

Subscriptions

Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, linked to your anonymous ID. We never see your payment card details.

Usage statistics

To improve the app we may record basic usage events with your anonymous ID, such as "scan completed" or which onboarding options were chosen, using PostHog’s EU servers. They never include photos, typed text, prices or crystal names you typed.

What we never do

We don’t sell or rent your data, show ads, track you across other apps or websites, or access your location, contacts or microphone.

Service providers

Supabase (servers and anonymous accounts), Anthropic (AI analysis), RevenueCat (subscription status), PostHog (usage statistics), and Apple and Google (app stores and payments). They may process data in the United States or the European Union.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices and rights

Settings → Delete all my data deletes everything stored on your phone, and on our server deletes your anonymous ID and its usage counts. Deleting the app removes what is on your phone. Your purchase history stays with Apple or Google and RevenueCat so your subscription keeps working; the app links it to your new anonymous ID. To ask for a copy of data linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have further rights, such as to access, correct or object to processing; contact us to use them.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. AI keys and other secrets are kept only on our server, never in the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
